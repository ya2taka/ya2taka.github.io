# Panopticon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This is an English translation of the [Japanese Privacy Policy](/panopticon/privacy/). If the two differ, the Japanese version prevails.

Panopticon (the "App") places live video from around the world on a map and helps you find hotels near what you see. There is no account registration, and the operator does not collect information that identifies you.

## 1. Information the App handles

| Information | Purpose | Where it is stored or sent |
|---|---|---|
| Current location (if you allow it) | To show the map from your current location | Used only on your device and not sent anywhere |
| Saved cameras | To list the cameras you saved | On your device only |
| That you agreed to the terms | To avoid showing the consent screen again | On your device only |
| Place search terms | To find places on the map | Sent to Apple's map search |
| Camera location (latitude and longitude), camera ID, video ID | To look up nearby hotels, stream status, and the current live video | Sent through the operator's relay server to Rakuten Web Service and YouTube |

Hotel searches use the camera's location, not your current location.

## 2. Relay server

The App retrieves hotels and stream status through a relay server that the operator runs on Cloudflare Workers. In addition to the information in section 1, information needed for communication (such as your IP address) reaches Cloudflare. The operator's program does not store IP addresses and does not create per-user records. Query results are stored temporarily for a short time to reduce repeated queries.

See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for how Cloudflare handles information.

## 3. YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. Video is played in the YouTube embedded player. By using the App, you are deemed to agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

- Player content comes directly from YouTube, and YouTube/Google may collect information using cookies and similar technologies. Content from third parties, including advertising providers, may be delivered on YouTube.
- Information is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The App does not request access to your Google account.

## 4. Rakuten Travel

Hotel information comes from Rakuten Web Service. Hotel booking links include affiliate links, and the operator may receive a referral fee when a booking is completed.

- Booking pages open in a browser inside the App (a Safari feature). Booking and payment take place on Rakuten Travel's pages, and the information you enter is handled under the Rakuten Group's policies.
- Rakuten may use cookies and similar technologies to measure referrals.
- The operator does not receive your name, contact details, or payment information.

## 5. Apple Maps

The App uses Apple's MapKit to display maps and search for places. Search terms and related information are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

## 6. Advertising and analytics

The App contains no SDKs for advertising or usage analytics. It does not track you across apps.

## 7. Changing permissions and deleting data

- You can change location permission at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security > Location Services > Panopticon.
- You can remove a saved camera at any time from its card.
- Deleting the App deletes all information stored on your device.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed at children.
