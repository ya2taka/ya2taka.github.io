# WatchMe

An energy level estimate that recovers with sleep and drops with activity, on iPhone, Apple Watch, and widgets. Talk about your day in a chat, and it becomes a diary entry. Your health data stays on your devices.

- [Privacy Policy](/watchme/privacy/en/) ([日本語](/watchme/privacy/))

## FAQ

**No data is showing**
In the Settings app, go to Health > Data Access & Devices > WatchMe and check that reading is allowed. After you wear your Apple Watch for a few days, the estimates become more stable.

**Voice diary isn't available**
The voice diary requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence and iOS 26 or later.

**I want to change ad tracking permission**
In the Settings app, go to Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can use every feature without allowing tracking.

**I want to delete my records**
You can delete diary entries from the diary screen in the app. Deleting the app deletes all records stored on your device.

**Can the numbers be used for diagnosis?**
No. WatchMe is not a medical device. The numbers are estimates to help you understand your own trends. If you are worried about your health, talk to a medical professional.
