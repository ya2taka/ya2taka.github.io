# WatchMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

WatchMe ("the app") is designed so that your health data, diary, and conversations stay on your own devices.

## 1. Information the app handles

| Information | How it's used | Where it's kept |
|---|---|---|
| Health data (steps, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, active energy, VO2 max, weight, body fat, workouts, and wrist temperature, respiratory rate, and blood oxygen during sleep) | To calculate and show trends and estimates such as energy and stress, to let you know on mornings when your body's readings are unusual, and to export your records when you choose | Read only; the app keeps no copy (calculated results stay on your device) |
| Tags, status modes, and earlier check-ins | To personalize calculations and compare tags with next-morning trends | On your device only |
| Habits (name, days per week, auto-check conditions, and the days you did them) | To show your progress, check habits off automatically from Health data and your diary, and compare days you did a habit with next-morning trends and that day's diary mood | On your device only |
| Diary (your chat, the diary text, your mood, and how your body felt as estimated from what you said) | To show your diary, and to personalize the energy estimate with how your body felt | On your device only |
| Talk (your chat, and the current feelings and body condition estimated from it) | To show your talk history, and to personalize the energy estimate with how your body felt | On your device only |
| Answers to the personality questions (Mini-IPIP) | To show your personality tendencies | On your device only |
| GHOST.md (short notes about you drawn from your diary and talks, plus items you write) | To show it, to give part of it to the diary and talk listener, and to export it when you choose | On your device only |
| Your voice in the diary and talks | Transcribed on your device | Never stored |
| Settings (notifications, appearance, etc.) | App settings | On your device only |

The app has no server, and the developer never receives any of the information above. Nothing is stored in iCloud.

## 2. Health (HealthKit)

- The app reads only the data types you allow. You can change this anytime in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > WatchMe.
- The app writes only the weight and body fat you enter, the mood you choose in your diary, and the current feelings estimated from a talk when you end it (both as State of Mind). The text of your talks is never written to Health.
- Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with third parties, including advertisers.

## 3. Apple Intelligence (on-device AI)

On supported devices, diary questions and text, talk replies and feeling estimates, and GHOST.md items are generated by Apple's on-device language model. Your diary, talks, and GHOST.md are never sent off your device (except when you export GHOST.md, see section 6, and when you use your own API key, described below). GHOST.md never infers your personality from conversations. The listener in Talk is an AI, not a medical professional.

### Using your own API key (AI Connection)

Only if you choose an AI service in Settings > AI Connection (Claude by Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini by Google, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you enter), agree to what will be sent, and save your own API key, the app has that service write the diary questions and diary text, and the talk replies and feeling estimates. This setting is off until you turn it on.

- What is sent: your conversation with the diary listener, your messages in Talk (up to the last 12 for replies and the last 24 for feeling estimates and for reading which habits you did), the GHOST.md items shared with the listener (when that GHOST.md setting is on), and the names of your habits that are not checked automatically from Health or your diary (when the diary asks about them and when reading which ones you did).
- What is never sent: Health data; feelings, tags, and state modes; the days you did your habits; your personality answers; voice recordings.
- It is sent only to the service you chose. It does not pass through any server of the developer, and the developer never receives it. The service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Usage is billed to your account with that service.
- Checking the connection and loading the model list never send your diary or talks.
- Your API key is stored only in your device's keychain and is not synced to iCloud Keychain. It is used only to send requests to the service you chose.
- You can switch back to "Off" at any time. To delete what was already sent, use that service.

## 4. Between your own devices

- Calculated values (such as your energy level) are sent directly to your paired Apple Watch.
- To keep your energy level and steps current between updates from your iPhone, the Apple Watch app reads the heart rate, active energy, sleep, and steps recorded on your watch and calculates on the watch. It never writes to Health.
- On iPad, the app reads iPhone data through Apple's Health iCloud sync, which you control.

## 5. Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

- For advertising, Google may collect device identifiers, your IP address, and information about ad views and taps. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
- You'll be asked whether to allow tracking the first time an ad is shown. If you don't allow it, you'll see non-personalized ads.
- In regions where the law requires consent, such as the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks before any ad is shown whether you agree to the use of data for ads. If you don't agree, ads that use data in a limited way are shown. No ads are shown until you answer. You can change your answer anytime in Settings > Ad Privacy Settings.
- Health data, your diary, talks, personality answers, GHOST.md, and check-ins are never included in ad requests or used for ad targeting.

## 6. Sharing

Only when you choose "Share to Another App" is your diary text passed to the app you pick.

Only when you choose Copy or Share under "Export" is GHOST.md, limited to the sections you select, passed to the app you pick. If you paste it into another AI service, it is sent to that service and handled under that service's privacy policy. The app never sends it on its own.

With "Export Records" in Settings, only when you pick a period and choose "Send by Email" or "Share or Print Another Way" are your records for that period made into a PDF and a CSV and passed to the person or app you choose (for example, to show a doctor at an appointment).

- Included: daily values from Health (resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps, VO2 max, weight, body fat, and wrist temperature, respiratory rate, and blood oxygen during sleep), energy and stress estimates, unwell and traveling periods, tags, and diary mood (5 levels).
- Not included: diary text, talks, GHOST.md, personality answers, habit records, or your voice.
- Email opens in iOS's mail composer, and you check the recipient and contents before sending it from your own email account. The recipient you type is not saved. Nothing passes through any server of ours, and we never receive the contents.
- Files you hand over remain on the recipient's devices and email services. The app never sends them on its own.

## 7. Deleting your data

- You can delete diary entries in the app.
- You can delete talks from "Talk History" in the Personality tab.
- You can delete a habit on its screen in the Habits tab. The days you checked off are deleted with it.
- You can delete personality answers on the Personality screen in the Personality tab.
- You can delete GHOST.md items one by one, or all at once, on the GHOST.md screen in the Personality tab. Text you've already exported must be deleted in the app or service you gave it to.
- Files made with "Export Records" are removed from a temporary location on your device each time they're remade. Files you've handed over must be deleted wherever you sent them.
- You can delete your own API key in Settings > AI Connection.
- Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
- Weight, body fat, moods, and feelings written to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed to children.
